\section{Light-cone quantization}
\label{sec:lcq}

We follow the conventions of Ref.~\cite{Hornbostel:1988fb} throughout.
Light-cone coordinates are $x^\pm = x^0 \pm x^1$, with $x^+$ the time.  The
mass-shell condition $p^+p^- = m^2$ makes $p^-$ the energy of a quantum
carrying longitudinal momentum $p^+ > 0$.  Positivity of $p^+$ is the
structural advantage of the scheme.  No combination of quanta with positive
momenta can add up to the vacuum's $p^+ = 0$, so the Fock vacuum is an
exact eigenstate of the full Hamiltonian.  Every basis state is built from
quanta that belong to the hadron, not to vacuum fluctuations.

Discretization places the theory in a box of length $2L$ in $x^-$ with
antiperiodic fermions, so momenta come in half-odd-integer units
$k^+_n = 2\pi n/L$.  The dimensionless resolution
\begin{equation}
  K \;=\; \frac{L}{2\pi}\,P^+
  \label{eq:Kdef}
\end{equation}
is a positive integer, and a basis state is a color-singlet partition of
$K$ among quanta.  (In code units we track $2K$, whose parity matches the
parton number.)  At $K = 3$ the available momentum fractions are the
partitions $(3)$, $(2,1)$ and $(1,1,1)$.  An equal-time discretization at
the same volume admits unbounded sets of positive and negative momenta.

The resolution is also the infrared regulator: the smallest momentum
fraction is $x = k/K \sim 1/K$.  The continuum limit $L \to \infty$ at
fixed $P^+$ is the single limit $K \to \infty$.  When the $1/K$ endpoint
cutoff dominates the error is the subject of Secs.~\ref{sec:budget} and
\ref{sec:chiral}.  Everything else --- mode expansions, constrained fields,
the construction of $P^-$ --- is unchanged from
Ref.~\cite{Hornbostel:1988fb}, and we do not repeat it.
