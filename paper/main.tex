\title{Light-cone-quantized QCD in 1+1 dimensions, revisited}

\author{Henry Lamm\,\orcidlink{0000-0003-3033-0791}}\email{hlamm@fnal.gov}\fnal

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We repeat the 1990 discretized light-cone calculation of SU($N$) gauge
theory in one space and one time dimension at resolutions up to
$2K = 100$, reproducing every published figure and table under a measured
four-component error budget.  The original Hamiltonian's weak-coupling
entries are limited by the momentum grid in a way no resolution repairs;
an improved Hamiltonian reaches $m/g = 2\times10^{-4}$, where the chiral
exponent comes out \numAlphaImproved{} against the grid artifact
\numAlphaStandard{} and the meson--baryon mass ratio lands on non-abelian
bosonization at the sub-percent level for $N \ge 5$.  Fock-sector
diagnostics identify sub-threshold four-quark and eight-parton states at
strong coupling, and the spectra supply continuum-extrapolated reference
values for tensor-network and quantum-simulation studies of the same
theories.
\end{abstract}
